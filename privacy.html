Privacy Policy for Tic Tac Toe

Last updated: August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tic Tac Toe ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Tic Tac Toe application.

Tic Tac Toe is a two-player game that supports local gameplay and online multiplayer using private game rooms.

1. Information We Collect

Local Gameplay

When you use Play Local, the game is played on your device.

You do not need to create an account to play a local game.

Local game information, such as the board state and current game status, is used to operate the game on your device.

Online Gameplay

When you use Play Online, the App uses Firebase services to provide online multiplayer functionality.

The App uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication to identify a player during an online game.

Firebase may provide the App with an anonymous user identifier associated with an anonymous Firebase account.

The App does not require you to provide your name, email address, phone number, password, or social media account to play online.

Game Information

When you create or join an online game, information processed may include:

  • Anonymous player identifier
  • Game room code
  • Player assignment, such as X or O
  • Game board state
  • Current player's turn
  • Game status
  • Winner information
  • Winning move information
  • Game creation and update timestamps

This information is used to operate and synchronize online multiplayer games.

2. Firebase

The App uses Firebase services provided by Google LLC, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.

Firebase Authentication is used to provide anonymous authentication for online multiplayer games.

Cloud Firestore is used to store and synchronize online game information between players.

Firebase may process information according to Google's privacy policies and the configuration of the Firebase services used by the App.

Google Privacy Policy

Firebase Privacy Information

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by the App is used to:

  • Provide local gameplay
  • Create online game rooms
  • Allow players to join online games
  • Identify players during an online game
  • Synchronize game states between players
  • Maintain game turns
  • Determine game results
  • Detect wins and draws
  • Provide the Play Again functionality
  • Troubleshoot technical problems
  • Maintain and improve the App

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information required to provide online multiplayer functionality is processed through Firebase services.

Information stored in an online game room may be accessible to the players participating in that game because this information is necessary for multiplayer functionality.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect our rights, users, or the security of the App.

5. Data Retention

Online game information may be stored in Firebase for the purpose of providing multiplayer functionality.

Game information may be removed when it is no longer required for the operation of the service or according to the configuration and retention practices of the Firebase project.

Anonymous Firebase authentication information may remain associated with an anonymous Firebase account until it is deleted or otherwise removed according to the applicable Firebase configuration and retention practices.

6. Children's Privacy

Tic Tac Toe is a general-audience game.

The App does not knowingly request names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other direct identifying information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information processed through the App.

Online multiplayer information is transmitted to Firebase using the security mechanisms provided by Firebase.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services necessary to provide certain functionality.

Google Firebase

Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore are used for online multiplayer functionality.

Google's Privacy Policy

Firebase Privacy Information

9. Your Choices

You may choose not to use the online multiplayer functionality.

You can continue to use the local game without creating a personal account.

If you do not want information associated with online gameplay to be processed through Firebase, you can choose not to use Play Online.

10. Data Deletion Requests

If you would like to request deletion of information associated with your use of the App, please contact us using the support email below.

When submitting a request, please provide enough information for us to understand the request.

Please do not include passwords, financial information, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

We may need to verify a request before taking action.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
